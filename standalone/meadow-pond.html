Meadow pond

A ten-by-ten-metre meadow with a pond, a spruce, an apple tree and a furnished log cabin you can walk into. A crackling fireplace, lamps and lanterns light it; slide the time into the night to see them glow. You are a free-floating camera: fly anywhere.
