Meadow pond

A meadow with a pond, a spruce, an apple tree and a furnished log cabin you can walk into, in a clearing of a hundred-by-hundred-metre valley ringed by forest. A crackling fireplace, lamps and lanterns light the cabin; slide the time into the night to see them glow. Walk the hills, or switch to flying.
